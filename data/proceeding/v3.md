## Caption

[1] BEFORE THE STATE PUBLIC UTILITY COMMISSION. In the Matter of the Commission's Rulemaking to Establish Interconnection Procedures for Energy Storage Resources. Docket No. 26-0412-RM. FINAL ORDER ADOPTING RULE.

## Background

[2] On November 4, 2025, the Commission opened this docket on its own motion to consider whether the interconnection procedures adopted in Docket No. 21-0187-RM adequately address the technical and procedural characteristics of energy storage resources.[^1] Since the adoption of those procedures, the number of interconnection requests for storage resources received by the state's electric utilities has increased from fewer than twenty per year to more than three hundred.

[3] The Commission held a technical workshop on January 15, 2026, attended by representatives of the state's electric utilities, storage developers, the Office of Consumer Counsel, and the Storage Developers Coalition. Workshop participants identified study timelines, application completeness review, queue transparency, and reporting as the areas most in need of revision.

[4] The Commission also reviewed interconnection data submitted by the utilities in response to its December 2, 2025 data request. That data showed a median study completion time of 71 business days for storage resources under 5 MW, against a median of 38 business days for solar resources of comparable size.[^2]

[5] The Commission received two rounds of written comments and held public hearings on April 22 and June 24, 2026. On the revised proposed rule, the utilities objected to the fixed daily penalty and to the cost of maintaining a public queue, and proposed that queue information instead be included in periodic reports. The Storage Developers Coalition supported the revised study timeline and asked that it be retained. Having considered the full record, the Commission adopts the rule set out below.

## Summary of Rule

[6] The rule establishes a defined study timeline for small storage resources, requires prompt notice of application deficiencies, establishes periodic reporting to the Commission, and establishes penalties for missed study deadlines.

[7] The rule applies to each electric utility subject to the Commission's jurisdiction and to interconnection requests for energy storage resources with a nameplate capacity of less than 5 MW, referred to in this rule as small storage resources. Interconnection requests for resources of 5 MW or greater remain subject to the procedures adopted in Docket No. 21-0187-RM.

## Ordering Paragraphs

[8] IT IS ORDERED that the following procedures are adopted.

[9] Definitions. "Business day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or state holiday. "Complete application" means an interconnection request that contains all information required by the utility's Commission-approved application form. "Small storage resource" means an energy storage resource with a nameplate capacity of less than 5 MW.

[10] Application review. The utility shall, within ten (10) business days after it receives an interconnection request for a small storage resource, provide the applicant written notice of any deficiencies in the request. A request for which no deficiency notice is issued within that period shall be deemed a complete application as of the date of receipt.

[11] Cure period. An applicant that receives a deficiency notice shall have thirty (30) days from the date of the notice to cure the identified deficiencies. A request that is not cured within that period shall be withdrawn from the queue without prejudice to refiling.

[12] Study completion. Each electric utility shall complete the interconnection study for a small storage resource within thirty (30) business days of the date of the complete application. The utility shall deliver the written study results to the applicant within three (3) business days of completing the study.

[13] Study scope. The interconnection study shall evaluate the effect of the proposed resource on the distribution system under both charging and discharging conditions, and shall identify any required system upgrades and the applicant's cost responsibility for those upgrades.

[14] Semi-annual reporting. Each electric utility shall file with the Commission, within thirty (30) days after the end of each June and December, a report stating the number of interconnection requests received, the number of studies completed, the median and maximum study completion time, the number of requests withdrawn, and a list of pending requests showing the date of receipt, the county, the nameplate capacity, and the current status of each.

[15] Extension. A utility that cannot complete a study within the period required by paragraph 12 may request an extension from the Commission's Director of Utility Engineering. The request shall state the reason for the delay and the expected completion date. An extension shall not exceed twenty (20) business days.

[16] Penalties. Where a utility fails to complete a study within the period required by paragraph 12, as extended under paragraph 15, the Commission may, after written notice to the utility and a cure period of fifteen (15) business days, assess a penalty not to exceed five hundred dollars ($500) for each business day the study remains incomplete after the cure period. Penalties assessed shall be paid to the state general fund and shall not be recovered from ratepayers.

[17] Existing requests. Interconnection requests for small storage resources pending on the effective date of this rule shall be subject to paragraph 12, with the period measured from the effective date.

## Compliance and Reporting

[18] Each electric utility shall file a compliance plan within sixty (60) days after the effective date of this rule describing the changes to its procedures, staffing, and systems required to comply.

[19] The Commission's Utility Engineering Division shall review the compliance plans and report to the Commission on the utilities' readiness within ninety (90) days after the effective date.

## Procedural Matters

[20] Effective date. This Order and the rule adopted herein are effective January 1, 2027.

[21] Rehearing. Any party may file an application for rehearing of this Order within thirty (30) days of the date of issuance, in accordance with the Commission's rules of practice.

[22] Further action. The Commission retains jurisdiction over this docket to address matters arising under the rule.

[23] This Order is issued by the Commission this 18th day of August, 2026.
