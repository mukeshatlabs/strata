## Caption

[1] BEFORE THE STATE PUBLIC UTILITY COMMISSION. In the Matter of the Commission's Rulemaking to Establish Interconnection Procedures for Energy Storage Resources. Docket No. 26-0412-RM. PROPOSED RULE AND NOTICE OF OPPORTUNITY FOR COMMENT.

## Background

[2] On November 4, 2025, the Commission opened this docket on its own motion to consider whether the interconnection procedures adopted in Docket No. 21-0187-RM adequately address the technical and procedural characteristics of energy storage resources.[^1] Since the adoption of those procedures, the number of interconnection requests for storage resources received by the state's electric utilities has increased from fewer than twenty per year to more than three hundred.

[3] The Commission held a technical workshop on January 15, 2026, attended by representatives of the state's electric utilities, storage developers, the Office of Consumer Counsel, and the Storage Developers Coalition.[^2] Workshop participants identified study timelines, application completeness review, queue transparency, and reporting as the areas most in need of revision.

[4] The Commission also reviewed interconnection data submitted by the utilities in response to its December 2, 2025 data request. That data showed a median study completion time of 71 business days for storage resources under 5 MW, against a median of 38 business days for solar resources of comparable size.[^3]

[5] The Commission thanks workshop participants and the utilities for their contributions to the record. This proposed rule reflects that record and the recommendations of Commission staff.

## Summary of Proposed Rule

[6] The proposed rule would establish a defined study timeline for small storage resources, require prompt notice of application deficiencies, require each utility to maintain a public interconnection queue, and establish periodic reporting to the Commission.

[7] The proposed rule applies to each electric utility subject to the Commission's jurisdiction and to interconnection requests for energy storage resources with a nameplate capacity of less than 5 MW, referred to in this rule as small storage resources. Interconnection requests for resources of 5 MW or greater remain subject to the procedures adopted in Docket No. 21-0187-RM.

## Ordering Paragraphs

[8] IT IS ORDERED that the following procedures are proposed for adoption.

[9] Definitions. "Business day" means any day other than a Saturday, Sunday, or state holiday. "Complete application" means an interconnection request that contains all information required by the utility's Commission-approved application form. "Small storage resource" means an energy storage resource with a nameplate capacity of less than 5 MW.

[10] Application review. Within ten (10) business days of receipt of an interconnection request for a small storage resource, the utility shall notify the applicant in writing of any deficiencies in the request. A request for which no deficiency notice is issued within that period shall be deemed a complete application as of the date of receipt.

[11] Cure period. An applicant that receives a deficiency notice shall have thirty (30) days from the date of the notice to cure the identified deficiencies. A request that is not cured within that period shall be withdrawn from the queue without prejudice to refiling.

[12] Study completion. Each electric utility shall complete the interconnection study for a small storage resource within forty-five (45) business days of the date of the complete application. The utility shall deliver the written study results to the applicant within five (5) business days of completing the study.

[13] Study scope. The interconnection study shall evaluate the effect of the proposed resource on the distribution system under both charging and discharging conditions, and shall identify any required system upgrades and the applicant's cost responsibility for those upgrades.

[14] Public queue. Each electric utility shall maintain on its public website a queue of pending interconnection requests for small storage resources. The queue shall identify, for each request, the date of receipt, the county, the nameplate capacity, and the current status. The utility shall update the queue no less than monthly.

[15] Quarterly reporting. Each electric utility shall file with the Commission, within thirty (30) days after the end of each calendar quarter, a report stating the number of interconnection requests received, the number of studies completed, the median and maximum study completion time, and the number of requests withdrawn during the quarter.

[16] Extension. A utility that cannot complete a study within the period required by paragraph 12 may request an extension from the Commission's Director of Utility Engineering. The request shall state the reason for the delay and the expected completion date. An extension shall not exceed twenty (20) business days.

[17] Existing requests. Interconnection requests for small storage resources pending on the effective date of this rule shall be subject to paragraph 12, with the period measured from the effective date.

## Compliance and Reporting

[18] Each electric utility shall file a compliance plan within sixty (60) days after the effective date of the final rule describing the changes to its procedures, staffing, and systems required to comply.

[19] The Commission's Utility Engineering Division shall review the compliance plans and report to the Commission on the utilities' readiness within ninety (90) days after the effective date.

## Procedural Matters

[20] Comments. Interested persons may file written comments on the proposed rule within thirty (30) days of the date of this notice. Reply comments may be filed within fifteen (15) days thereafter.

[21] Public hearing. The Commission will hold a public hearing on the proposed rule on April 22, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. in the Commission's hearing room.

[22] Further action. Following the comment period and hearing, the Commission may adopt the proposed rule, adopt a revised rule, or take other action as the record warrants.

[23] This notice is issued by the Commission this 10th day of March, 2026.
